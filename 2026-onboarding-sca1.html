Onboarding of SCCA I

Onboarding of Newly Hired School Counselor Associate I


Schedule


Batch Date Venue
1 only October 26, 27, 28, 2026 Queen Maricel Inn, San Isidro Village, Virac, Catanduanes

Learning Outcomes

At the end of the training, participants are expected to:

  • Orient the newly hired SCCAI on the Department of Education's vision, mission, core values, organizational structure, and policies;
  • Familiarize them with their roles, responsibilities, key result areas, scope of practice, and essential competencies as School Counselor Associate I; and;
  • Strengthen their capacity to effectively deliver learner support services and perform other functions in accordance with professional and ethical standards.

Target Participants

The profile of this participants:

  • Newly hired School Counselor Associates I
  • School Head of SCCA I (Day 2 only)
