Learning and Development for Newly-Hired Teachers

Bathc 1 - September 12, 13, 19, 2026

Bathc 2 - September 20, 26, 27, 2026

Queen Maricel Inn, San Isidro Village, Virac


Objectives

▪ Orient the newly hired teachers on roles, functions, duties, and responsibilities of a public school teacher.

▪ Apply knowledge, skills, attitude, and values, required of teachers as specified in
the Philippine Professional Standards for Teachers (PPST) and DepEd Core Values.

▪ Improve practice towards career advancement based on set professional development goals.


Target Participants:

Newly-hired teachers with 0-3 years of experience
