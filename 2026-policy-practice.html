Learning Outcomes

At the end of the training, participants are expected to:

  • Develop quality annotated lesson plans integrating research-based instructional strategies, including Differentiated Instruction and Universal Design for Learning (UDL);
  • Strengthen the application of research-informed teaching strategies to improve instructional practices; and;
  • Apply research-based and inclusive teaching strategies in their daily instruction to improve students' literacy and numeracy achievement as evidenced by increased learning outcomes.
